Seals

Push-Type Seals

19 & 32 mm

Closed push-type seals for steel strapping, galvanized for rust resistance. They work with many manual and pneumatic strapping machines and hold with a shear strength of roughly 80 kg/mm² at the seal area.

Features

  • Closed (push-type) seal for steel strap.
  • Galvanized for rust resistance.
  • Works with many manual and pneumatic strapping machines.
  • Holding strength ≈ 80 kg/mm² at the seal area.
