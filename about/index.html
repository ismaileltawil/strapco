About Strapco

Egypt’s original strapping manufacturer.

Strapco (El Tawil for Straps Production) was founded in 1990 as the first Egyptian company to produce steel and plastic strap packages. More than three decades on, we remain a pioneer, specialised in manufacturing steel and plastic strapping in every size, together with the seals each size requires.

One supplier, the full range

From light polypropylene for carton bundling to heavy steel for the toughest loads, our strapping serves wide sectors of Egyptian industry, and the seals and tooling that go with them come from the same place.

Everything is produced and quality-checked at our plant in the 2nd Industrial Zone, New Borg El Arab, Alexandria, and shipped from our office in the heart of the city.

Quality

Made to standard, tested in our lab

Our strapping is produced under EN-ISO 9001:2015 and TÜV, and carries a Quality Certificate License from the Egyptian Organization for Standardization and Quality (ES 2330/2007). Steel is tested with modern quality-control methods in our own laboratory.

  • EN-ISO 9001:2015
  • TÜV
  • ES 2330/2007
  • Signode-compatible tooling
