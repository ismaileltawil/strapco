01About Strapco

Egypt’s first strapping manufacturer.

Strapco, El Tawil for Straps Production, was the first Egyptian company to produce steel and plastic strap packages. More than thirty years on, we remain a pioneer, specialised in manufacturing plastic and steel strapping in different sizes, and the seals required for each size.

02

One supplier

From light polypropylene for carton bundling to heavy steel for the toughest loads, our strapping serves wide sectors of industry. The seals each size needs come from the same place.

Strapping is produced at our plant in the 2nd Industrial Zone, New Borg El Arab, and our office is in Mansheya, in the centre of Alexandria.
