The plant

Every coil is produced at our plant in the 2nd Industrial Zone, New Borg El Arab, Alexandria. Steel strapping is tested with modern quality-control methods in our own laboratory.

For more than thirty years we have specialised in steel and plastic strapping in different sizes, and the seals required for each size.
